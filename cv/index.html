Summary Associate Scientist at CIMAS and NOAA AOML. I study how ocean warming, acidification, diseases, and land-based pollution affect corals and the coral-algal symbiosis, and which environmental and genetic factors enhance coral resilience. I combine field data, controlled experiments, and molecular techniques, and apply these findings to coral restoration and reef management.
